Key Analytical & Statistical Findings

Logistics SLA Impact on Customer Satisfaction (Welch's T-Test & Mann-Whitney U):On-time deliveries average 4.29 stars ($n = 89,443$), whereas late deliveries drop to 2.27 stars ($n = 6,381$)—a -2.02 star rating collapse ($t = 100.74, p < 0.0001$).

B2B Seller Acquisition Funnel (Two-Proportion Z-Test):Across 8,000 MQLs and 842 Closed-Won sellers (10.53% overall win rate), Paid Search converts at 12.30% vs. Organic Search at 11.80% (+4.17% relative lift). However, the difference is not statistically significant ($Z = 0.4636, p = 0.6429$), indicating opportunity to optimize Paid Search CAC.

Experimental Design for Retention (Statistical Power Analysis):Lifting Olist's 3.0% baseline repeat purchase rate to 4.2% (+40% relative lift) via a post-delivery loyalty voucher requires 3,758 randomly assigned customers per variant (7,516 total) at 80% power ($\alpha = 0.05$).

Revenue Leakage & Data Governance Audit:Identified R$ 269.74K in canceled/unavailable order leakage (~1,230 orders) while maintaining 98.99% average pipeline completeness.
